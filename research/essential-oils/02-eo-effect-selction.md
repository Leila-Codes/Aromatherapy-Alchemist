# Essential Oil Effects

Reviewing literature including "Essential oils for clinical aromatherapy: A comprehensive review" (Lalitkumar K. Vora, et. al, 2024)

The following benefits were reported:
- Improve sleep symptoms 
- Balanced sleep 
- Improved quality of life in working women
- Improved deep sleep, decreased rapid-eye movement sleep and increased first wake latency after sleep
- Decreases anxiety symptoms 
- Improved VAS score for anxiety, normalized heart rate, blood
pressure
- Citrus oil comparatively less effective in female to suppress
anxiety symptoms
- Decrease anxiety symptoms and better perioperative condition
management
- Reduced antiemetic requirements

Most of these, reported from "Olfactory stimulation" which is the primary focus on the app though "Muscle relaxation" were included.

## Findings Overview
Reviewing these sources, there's too many broad and overlapping claims across the essential oil's to suitably group their effects into quantifiable categories and/or potencies. 

This has stumped me for quite some time, however further online research and suggestions has now suggested that we may have better luck by classifying the oil's using their compound elements.
